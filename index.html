06 / FAIRI CONNECTS
06 / 09

It remembers the child.

Fairi can remember a child's interests, understand changes in behaviour, and help parents approach difficult situations with more context.

A SCHOOL DAY
Fairi → Parent “Aaj Aarav thoda upset lag raha hai. School mein kuch hua hai shayad. Waise usko drawing bahut pasand hai, aur drawing karte time woh usually relax karta hai.”
Parent → Fairi “Tum usse thoda baat karwa sakti ho? Agar woh comfortable ho toh mujhe bata de ki school mein kya hua.”
Fairi → Child “Heyy Aarav, aaj school mein kuch hua kya? Agar abhi baat nahi karni toh it's okay. Main yahin hoon.”
Child → Fairi “Actually… meri drawing ko class mein kisi ne funny bola.”
Fairi → Child “Ohh… that probably hurt. Tum chaho toh mummy ko bata sakte ho. I think woh tumhari side samjhegi.”
Fairi → Parent “Usne bataya ki class mein kisi ne uski drawing ka mazaak banaya. Aap pehle uski feelings ko validate karna, phir calmly poochna ki exactly kya hua.”
Parent “Okay. Main usse calmly baat karungi.”
