01 / ABOUT

Understand the child behind the screen.

Fairi is a context-aware Guardian AI designed to turn digital wellbeing from restriction into guidance.

Fairi is being developed as a persistent, child-friendly AI companion that lives alongside the child's digital experience.

Instead of treating every digital interaction as a rule violation, Fairi is designed to understand context, communicate naturally and eventually guide the child toward safer and healthier choices.

The central philosophy is simple:

“Fairi doesn't just control what a child does on a device. It understands the child behind the screen.”
CORE PHILOSOPHY UNDERSTAND PROTECT REDIRECT CONNECT
02 / THE GUARDIAN AI IDEA

More than an animated companion.

These are the four principles Fairi is being built around. They describe the product vision, not a claim that every capability is already complete.

01

Understand

Interpret digital context such as activity, duration, intent, preferences and relevant memory.

02

Protect

Identify situations that may eventually require safety guidance or intervention.

03

Redirect

Instead of only saying “stop”, Fairi is designed to suggest a constructive alternative.

04

Connect

Fairi is designed to act as a bridge between child and parent rather than replacing either one.

03 / SCOPE

Small prototype. Bigger Guardian AI vision.

The prototype proves the interaction foundation. The larger Guardian AI system is being built in stages.

CURRENT

What Fairi is today

An experimental Android companion that establishes the core Fairi interaction loop.

  • Floating Fairi character
  • Voice interaction and listening
  • AI-generated responses
  • Text-to-speech
  • Experimental live context / monitoring
  • Initial Fairi Brain architecture
FUTURE

What Fairi is growing into

A mature context-aware Guardian AI that can guide, protect and connect without becoming a surveillance or punishment system.

  • Reliable inappropriate-content detection
  • Real safety interventions
  • Screen-time enforcement and restrictions
  • Secure local/cloud memory architecture
  • Parent ecosystem and contextual insights
  • Advanced personalization and wider platform support
04 / HOW IT WORKS

Context becomes guidance.

Fairi's intended intelligence loop combines context, memory and AI before generating a response.

01

Context

Relevant activity, interaction and available context enter the Fairi system.

02

Memory

Relevant preferences and interactions can provide personalization.

03

AI Brain

The AI layer interprets the available information and generates an appropriate response.

04

Response

Fairi communicates through voice, text, UI and eventually contextual actions.

→
